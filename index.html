Print & image calculators that run right in your browser

No sign-up, no uploads, no waiting. Work out print sizes, pixel dimensions, paper sizes, aspect ratios and DPI conversions with simple, accurate tools.

Print Size Calculator

Convert pixel dimensions to inches and centimetres at any DPI, or find the pixel size you need for a target print.

Paper Size to Pixels

Find out how many pixels an A4, A3, Letter or Legal page is at 72, 150, 300 DPI and more.

Aspect Ratio Calculator

Simplify any width and height to a ratio, compare it with standard ratios, and scale dimensions proportionally.

Pixels to Inches / CM Converter

Convert pixels to physical size and back again using the DPI or PPI of your image.

Retina Image Size Calculator

Generate @1x, @2x and @3x asset dimensions from one base size, plus an uncompressed file-size estimate.

Why these tools exist

Small production questions should not require an account, an app download or a paid subscription. Every calculator on this site runs locally in your browser: your images and numbers never leave your device. More tools are added regularly.
