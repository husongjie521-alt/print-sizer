How it works

The image is read entirely in your browser: width ÷ DPI gives the physical print width. A 6000 × 4000 px image prints at 20 × 13.3 in at 300 DPI, 30 × 20 in at 200 DPI, and 40 × 26.7 in at 150 DPI. Larger prints are possible at lower DPI but will look soft up close.

Frequently asked questions

Is my image uploaded to a server?

No. All processing happens in your browser with JavaScript. The file never leaves your device.

Why does my print service say my photo is too small?

Print services usually set a minimum DPI for each product. Compare their required pixel dimensions with the image size shown here; if your image has fewer pixels, lower the print size or DPI until it fits.
